**Code:** [github.com/PSRahul/facial-keypoint-cnn](https://github.com/PSRahul/facial-keypoint-cnn)

This deep learning model, based on convolutional neural networks, detects the left eye, right eye and mouth in an image of a face.

The model was built in PyTorch with an MSE loss and the AdaGrad optimizer.
